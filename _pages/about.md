I am a doctoral researcher in Artificial Intelligence at Yonsei University, working on **LLM reasoning and post-training**. My research focuses on how language models can acquire and improve reasoning capabilities through better training signals, feedback, and iterative refinement.

My work spans **reasoning model training, reinforcement-learning-based post-training, knowledge distillation, synthetic and model-generated data, self-reflection, latent reasoning, and feedback-driven self-correction.** My earlier research also includes structured and formal language generation, such as Text-to-SQL and molecular sequence generation.

I expect to complete my Ph.D. in **February 2027** and am interested in **Research Engineer, Research Scientist, and Applied Scientist** opportunities involving model training, reasoning, post-training, and model capability improvement.

:rocket: Research
======
**:thought_balloon: LLM Reasoning & Post-Training**
* [GeneralThinker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.27934) — RL-based post-training for domain-general reasoning with dense reward signals and fine-grained credit assignment.
* [SpiralThinker](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-acl.1605/) — Iterative latent reasoning through text–latent interleaving and progressive representation alignment.
* [TinyThiner](https://aclanthology.org/2025.naacl-long.309/) — Reasoning knowledge distillation with synthetic reasoning data and iterative self-reflection.
  
**:pencil2: Structured / Formal Language Generation**
* [LitE-SQL](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-eacl.186/) — Lightweight Text-to-SQL with supervised fine-tuning, execution-guided preference optimization, and self-correction.
* [SELF-EdiT](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10489-023-04915-8) — Transformer-based molecular editing with SELFIES for structure-constrained molecular optimization.
